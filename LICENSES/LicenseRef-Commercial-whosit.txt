WHY IT IS READABLE AT ALL

Because hiding it would make the product worse without making it safer. What lives
here is the retainable verification audit trail — the component that records who
proved what and when, keeps that record, and exports it. Organisations buy it
precisely because they must be able to evidence identity checks, and an organisation
that has to evidence something needs to inspect the code its evidence rests on.
A closed audit trail is a weaker audit trail.

The protection is therefore legal, not technical. There is no licence key, no
activation and nothing to circumvent.
