Apple Worldwide Developer Relations Intermediate Certificate (G4)

This is not a software licence. `assets/pass/AppleWWDRCAG4.pem` is the PUBLIC
intermediate CA certificate published by Apple Inc. It is required to build and
verify Apple Wallet passes (.pkpass) and is distributed by Apple for exactly that
purpose.

It contains no secret. The matching private key is NOT part of this repository;
it belongs to the operator's own Apple Developer account.
